# Image Generation Prompt: Tend Pets iOS Premium Mockup

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: premium iOS app design mockup board
Primary request: Create a high-fidelity iOS app design mockup image for "Tend Pets", a premium pet medication and care tracker app. Show five iPhone screens side by side on a clean studio background: Today, Pet Profile, Add Care, Records, and Plus Paywall.

Scene/backdrop:
A refined product design presentation board, warm off-white background, soft natural shadows, no decorative blobs or gradients. The image should look like a polished App Store / Figma concept presentation for a paid iOS app.

Subject:
Five realistic iPhone mockups, front-facing, evenly spaced but with slight natural overlap and depth. Each phone shows one app screen.

Style/medium:
High-fidelity native iOS SwiftUI-style interface, premium mobile UI design, crisp typography, SF Pro-like system type, subtle iOS materials, elegant spacing, calm and trustworthy healthcare-adjacent feel.

Design direction:
Calm Clinical Companion. The app should feel clean, trustworthy, quiet, warm, and worth paying for. It must not feel childish, generic SaaS, overly cute, or hospital-cold.

Color palette:
Warm off-white #FAF9F6 background, white surfaces, deep charcoal text #1F2420, muted secondary text #6E746F, sage green primary #2F6F5E, medicine blue #3E6FB6, food amber #B8792D, visit/vaccine violet #7A5DB5, subtle alert amber/red only where needed.

Memorable visual anchor:
Use a "Care Ring" around pet photos: a thin circular progress ring showing today's care completion. Use it consistently on Today, Pet Profile, and Widget-like elements. It should be elegant and recognizable.

Screen 1: Today
- Large title: "Today"
- Date row and overall progress
- Horizontal pet carousel with pet photos and Care Rings
- Main card: "Momo's heart med" / "1 tablet, after breakfast" / "Due now"
- Clear primary button: "Done"
- Secondary actions: "Snooze", "Skip"
- Smaller list items for food, weight check, and vet visit

Screen 2: Pet Profile
- Pet photo with Care Ring
- Name: "Momo"
- Species and age
- Current weight
- Sections: "Active meds", "Next visit", "Weight trend", "Recent notes"
- Include a clean small line chart for weight trend

Screen 3: Add Care
- Title: "Add Care"
- Segmented choices: Medicine, Food, Weight, Visit
- Form fields: Pet, Name, Time, Repeat
- A time picker-like row
- Primary button: "Save reminder"
- Keep it minimal and easy, not a long medical form

Screen 4: Records
- Title: "Records"
- Filter chips: Medicine, Weight, Vaccine, Visit
- Timeline list with clean dated records
- A card labeled "Vet Summary"
- Button: "Export PDF"
- Include a small chart or document preview

Screen 5: Plus Paywall
- Title: "Keep every routine organized"
- Subtext: "For pets with daily care, shared routines, and vet-ready records."
- Feature bullets with small iOS-style icons:
  "Unlimited reminders"
  "Up to 5 pets"
  "Vet summary export"
  "Cloud backup"
- Plan selector: "$4.99 monthly" and "$39.99 yearly"
- Primary CTA: "Start 7-day free trial"
- Secondary: "Continue with Free"
- Restore Purchase and Terms/Privacy in small text

Composition/framing:
Landscape presentation image. Five phone screens should be readable, with the Today screen slightly emphasized in the center or front. Use realistic iPhone proportions. Leave a small top label "Tend Pets" on the presentation board, but keep text minimal.

Lighting/mood:
Soft studio lighting, premium product shot, subtle shadows under phones, calm and polished.

Constraints:
- Must look like real iOS app screens, not a website.
- Use clear app UI structure and realistic mobile spacing.
- Avoid cartoon pet illustrations; use tasteful small pet photos or realistic avatar placeholders.
- Avoid purple-blue SaaS gradients.
- Avoid oversized marketing hero layouts inside the app screens.
- Avoid clutter and tiny unreadable UI.
- No fake browser chrome.
- No Android UI.
- No watermark.
- No extra logos beyond "Tend Pets".
- Text should be as clean and legible as possible; if exact text is hard, prioritize correct layout and visual quality.
