Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Overview

Tend Pets helps people track pet medications, visits, vaccines, weight, meals, and care notes. This policy explains what data is handled by the app.

Data You Enter

The app may store pet names, species, medication names, care times, dose notes, weight entries, food notes, visit notes, vaccine records, and completion history. This data is used to show reminders, records, and vet summaries inside the app.

Local Storage

The current app stores care data on the user's device. If cloud backup or family sharing is added before release, this policy and the App Store privacy answers must be updated before submission.

Notifications

If the user enables reminders, the app schedules local notifications for care times. Notification permission can be changed in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Tend Pets does not receive full payment card details.

Medical Boundary

Tend Pets is not a veterinary service. It does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend dosage, or provide emergency guidance.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@tendpets.app.
