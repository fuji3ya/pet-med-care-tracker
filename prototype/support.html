Support

How can we help?

For app support, billing questions, privacy requests, or review questions, contact:

support@tendpets.app

Before contacting support

  • Check that notifications are enabled in iOS Settings.
  • Confirm the care time and repeat setting in Tend Pets.
  • For subscriptions, open iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Medical note

Tend Pets cannot answer veterinary questions. Contact a veterinarian for diagnosis, dosage, treatment, or emergency care.
